# Intent/base study: protocol

> Rewritten after the first draft was scoped wrong. That draft was a measurement study
> — corpus extraction, per-instance data, numeric falsifiers — for a question that is
> about **trend**, not distinct data. The measurement apparatus is gone. What survives
> from it: the survival filter, the out-of-scope list, the traps, and the abandon
> condition.

## The decision this serves

The lattice (`2^a * 3^b * 5^c`, `b,c <= 1`) is mechanics: it is why layouts compose, it
is identical at every base, it ships. `--spacing-base` is the free parameter and the
only spacing parameter that changes what a system looks like. Base 4 is assigned to
**friendly** by operator ruling — it is the current web's base and the current web's
register.

Per the motion doctrine, an intent preset is a value-set over the system tokens applied
by Studio, never encoded in the system. The deliverable is a table, not a generator
change.

## What this study is

**A read of the arc, not a census.** The question is where design has been over roughly
a century, what direction it is moving, and where we are on that curve. Individual
artifacts are evidence for the shape; they are not the subject.

This matters because the shape is the thing that has predictive value. A measured base
unit from 1957 tells you what one studio did. The observation that rigid systems
produce a backlash against *permission* rather than against *geometry*, and that the
geometry returns intact afterward, tells you what to build.

## What we need to answer

1. **What is the arc?** The movements, in order, with what each was reacting against.
   Bauhaus/De Stijl -> Swiss -> New Wave/postmodern -> Memphis -> digital minimalism ->
   the current return.
2. **What is the period?** The Swiss dominance ran roughly 1950-1972 before Weingart
   broke it from inside; the rejection ran to the mid-90s; the digital return began
   ~2014 with Material. If the cycle has a period, we are ~12 years into the current
   phase. Does the spacing between turns hold, or is it accelerating?
3. **What recurs, and what does not?** This is the survival filter and it is the point.
   A register that returns after being rejected has something behind it. One that
   appears once was fashion. The grid returned; Memphis did not.
4. **What is each recurring register *for*?** Stated as emotional and functional
   content, not adjectives. Swiss neutrality was explicitly the removal of the
   designer, sought as objectivity. That is a stance about authority, and it is why the
   backlash was moral rather than aesthetic.
5. **Where does the turn go from here?** Sean's read is a break against current American
   tastes and their emotional register. The study's job is to give that read enough
   grounding to argue with — what the current register encodes, what its opposite is,
   and which prior turn it most resembles.

## What counts as an answer

A characterization of the arc with enough specificity to place a register on it, and an
assignment of registers to bases that follows from where they sit.

Each register in the output needs:

- what it was reacting against
- its emotional/functional content
- whether it recurred, and how many times
- the direction it moved spacing: tighter or looser than what it displaced

That last field is the one that matters for the base, and it is comparative rather than
absolute. "Looser than Swiss" is recoverable and useful; "18px" is neither.

## The one discipline to keep from the first draft

**Find where competing readings diverge, before committing to one.** This is what
settled the musical question — consonance and lineage agreed everywhere except the
value 36, and that case decided it. For a trend claim the equivalent is: identify a
transition the reading did *not* come from, and check whether it behaves as predicted.
A shape fitted to the whole century and then confirmed on the whole century has not
been tested.

## Explicitly out of scope

Do not re-derive these. Each was tested to destruction in the #2031 thread; see
`spacing-field-study.md` and `grid-lineage.md`.

- **Musical ratios as structure.** No mechanism transfers from auditory consonance;
  where consonance and lineage diverge, consonance predicts wrong.
- **Field surveys as normative.** A census of shipped sites measures one moment of one
  register. The same method would have endorsed the 8pt grid in 1972.
- **Weber-Fechner for spacing.** Stevens' exponent for visual length is ~1.0.
- **Density as register, measured on current sites.** Between/within variance 0.16, and
  if everyone shares base 4 that null is the expected result.

## Traps

1. **A beautiful shape is more suspect, not less.** Three tidy models were fitted and
   discarded in the 2026-07 intent study — a monotone ramp, a sinusoidal ring, a latent
   "commitment" variable — each of which fit the data and was wrong. The same failure
   recurred here with a musical chord. The pattern is not a coincidence: tidy structure
   is what I reach for when I do not have an answer.
2. **Run the refutation on the reading you like**, not only the one you doubt.
3. **Do not narrate a cycle into existence.** Four turns over a century is a small
   number of events; any curve fits four points. The claim that survives must be about
   *mechanism* — why rigidity produces backlash — not about periodicity.
4. **Defer to the perceptual read and verify it, do not overwrite it with a prettier
   model.** Recorded from the motion study: the operator's shape-read beat my
   curve-fitting every time it mattered.

## What would abandon the premise

- The recurring registers differ on weight, contrast and ornament, with spacing along
  for the ride. **The most likely outcome and not a bad one** — it relocates intent to
  the parameters that carry it and scopes spacing back to mechanics with one base.
- The arc has no mechanism, only a sequence. A list of movements in order is history,
  not a basis for choosing a base.
- The turns are driven by production technology rather than sentiment — photo-typesetting,
  desktop publishing, the browser, the design-system era. If the cycle is tooling, then
  the next turn is predicted by what tooling changes, and rafters *is* a tooling change,
  which is a different and more actionable story.

## Division of labour

Assembling the history and characterizing the movements is mine. **The read of where
the turn goes is the operator's** — it is a perceptual judgment about current sentiment,
which is the one input I have no instrument for, and the motion study is on record that
my instrument loses to it.

Values arrive from studies, never invented. Base 4 = friendly is assigned and evidenced.
The remaining assignments follow from the arc or they do not get made.
